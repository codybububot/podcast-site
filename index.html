EP 02

The One Thing AI Can't Change | Levy Cheng × FateTell

In an era when every AI founder is sprinting, Levy Cheng sits unusually still. He's the founder of FateTell — an AI app combining LLMs with Chinese fate-reading (命理), targeting overseas markets. In this episode: why reasoning models finally made AI fortune-telling accurate, the 'millennial need' thesis, the car-and-road metaphor for fate vs. destiny, and a live Jiaobeï divination throw.
